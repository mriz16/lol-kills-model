# Goblin lines and side balance (28,974 player-series since 2025-06-01, data through 2026-10-24)

Walk-forward, live locked model with S. PP-like line = trailing-10 market mean to the nearest x.5. Goblin lines = that line minus 1..4 kills. Raw = the S model's P(more); G = the app's goblin P (raw, supports through their beta map, refit weekly).

Goblin breakeven = (1 / 8.25) ^ (1/6) = 70.3% (6-pick goblin Power at 8.25x); a goblin PLAY needs P >= 73.3% (breakeven + 3 pts).
