# Backtest: real Oracle's Elixir data (walk-forward, 20,000 sims)

Run: `python scripts/run_backtest.py --tag real` on the parquet from the refresh of
2026-10-13 (after the LTA N -> LCS alias). 75-day warmup, every later series projected
from prior data only; `p_map` from walk-forward Elo. Same 22,073 player-series scored for
every variant, so all `crps_vs_base` numbers are paired.

## Recommendation: lock `shrink30 + A + B50`

`EngineConfig(shrink_games=30, position_kappa=True, neff_floor=50)`, game-script OFF,
smooth bootstrap (C) OFF.

1. **B (n_eff floor) is the real gain.** shrink30+B50 vs shrink30: CRPS -0.0130
   (se 0.0017, ~8 se). Total vs base -0.0199 (se 0.0018, ~11 se); log score
   2.4872 -> 2.4715. Better than base in 9 of 10 leagues; the only loss is FST
   (+0.039, se 0.025, n=130, 1.5 se: noise-level, but watch it).
2. **Floor 50 > 35 > 25, but it is flattening**: B35-B25 -0.0011 (se 0.0003),
   B50-B35 -0.0004 (se 0.0003). 50 is the best tested; higher floors were not tested
   (see open issues).
3. **A (per-position kappa) is CRPS-neutral but improves calibration.** A+B50 vs B50:
   CRPS -0.0002 (se 0.0002). It lowers ECE at the median line 0.0226 -> 0.0203 and at
   half-point lines 0.0168 -> 0.0152, and does the same in every pairing
   (shrink30 vs +A, B25/B35/B50 vs +A). For pricing over/unders, calibration is what
   matters, so it stays in. If you want the simplest config, shrink30+B50 is within
   noise on CRPS.
4. **shrink30 over shrink20**: -0.0067 (se 0.0007), consistent.
5. **C (smooth bootstrap) rejected**: CRPS beats shrink30 (-0.0033) but loses to B50 by
   0.0097 (se 0.0017), and it roughly doubles ECE (0.035 median line, 0.030 half-point):
   it fattens the tails by pushing too much mass out of the middle.

## Data issues / open items

- **League codes**: OE files the 2025 LCS as `LTA N` (2026 is `LCS` again). Aliased to
  LCS in `src/config.py`; +214 games / 93 series. Not aliased (out of scope):
  `LTA S`, `LTA` (N-vs-S cross-conference), `EWC`, `KeSPA Cup`.
- **LPL 2025 missing**: 931 of 1,479 LPL games are not `complete` in OE (all of 2025),
  so LPL history starts in 2026. The complete-only rule is locked; if the partial
  rows carry kills/teamkills, including them would need your sign-off.
- Team alias table updated to OE 2026 names (Dplus Kia, Kiwoom DRX, HANJIN BRION,
  DN SOOPers, GiantX).
- **Mean bias**: every variant under-predicts by ~0.13 kills per player-series
  (top -0.25, mid -0.18, bot -0.17, jng -0.06, sup -0.01 for the recommended config).
  Kill environment is rising faster than the 0.65/patch decay tracks, or the opponent
  adjustment is too timid. This is a bigger, cheaper target than more tail work.
- **Tails still thin**: at median+1.5..3.5, hit rate / predicted = 1.10 (base 1.11);
  top 1.17, mid 1.10, bot 1.11, jng 1.04, sup 1.03. B narrows it slightly. Much of
  this is the mean bias above, so fix that first.
- **Low P(over) buckets under-predict**: when the model says < 25% at the rounded
  median, hits come in ~10 pts higher (n ~ 360). Those are the props most likely to
  look like "unders with edge" -- treat with caution until the bias is fixed.
- Higher n_eff floors (75/100) and a mean recalibration were not tested.
