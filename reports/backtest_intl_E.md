# International-event backtest (data through 2026-10-23, 10000 sims)

ECE at the line = rounded median (`ece_median`) and at half-point lines median +/- 0.5, 1.5 (`ece_half`), 5% buckets. `ece_median_null` = the ECE a perfectly calibrated model would show at the same sample size (outcomes drawn from its own P(over), 200 draws): ECE below it is noise, not miscalibration.
